THE SOC SLA CLOCK

One incident produces one elapsed timer on the wall clock — but nine lifecycle stages, grouped into five separately-measured contractual SLA clocks, plus two pre-SLA telemetry stages the SOC does not own.

Pre-SLA telemetry latency
Detection, Ingestion — network & tooling delay before the SOC's contractual clock even starts. Not owned by the SOC.
Reading this diagram

"Meeting the SLA" almost never means one thing. Each colored arc is measured, reported, and penalized independently — a case can beat its Acknowledgement SLA and still blow its Containment SLA.
