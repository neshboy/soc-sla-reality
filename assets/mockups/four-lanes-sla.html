Four SLAs, One Word
One P1 incident, read through four different documents — a training manual, a contract, a ticketing tool, and a customer's expectations. Each one calls itself “the SLA.”
1WHAT TRAINING TEACHES
BOOK SLA
Alert → Investigate → Escalate → Close. Clean phases, one analyst, complete evidence, textbook order.
2THE NEGOTIATED LEGAL TEXT
CONTRACTUAL SLA
P1 acknowledge in 30 min. Severity definitions, clock start/stop conditions, penalties, exclusions.
3WHAT THE TOOLING MEASURES
OPERATIONAL SLA
Ticket stage field, automation-rule “SLA Exceeded” trigger, queue-order magnitude score — a looser proxy for the contract.
4UNWRITTEN, OFTEN UNREALISTIC
CUSTOMER EXPECTATION
Customer hears “30-minute response” and assumes “resolved,” not “acknowledged.”
The catch

Same incident. Four different readings of “the SLA.”
